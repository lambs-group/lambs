### H2: Fibre infrastructure, built by a family-run team since 1988
Lambs Group builds and tests fibre networks for national operators and their delivery partners. We survey the route, design it, install the duct and cable, splice and test it, and reinstate the ground afterwards. Our crews work on full-fibre-to-the-home and premises programmes across the North West, Yorkshire and the West Midlands, including Virgin Media's Motif rollout in partnership with Fujitsu. We are based in Warrington and have been delivering infrastructure since 1988.

### H2: How a fibre build runs with us
Every programme follows the same stages, so you always know what happens next. Our [How We Deliver](/how-we-deliver) page covers the full process; for telecoms work it looks like this.

- **Survey.** We walk the route before we quote. Underground and overhead routes are checked, marked and photographed, so problems are found on paper and not in the trench.
- **Design.** We plan the route, the duct and cable specification, and the programme, working to the operator's design rules.
- **Build.** Our crews install duct and cable using the right method for the ground: mole ploughing across verges and rural routes, directional drilling under roads, driveways and gardens, and microduct blowing for fibre.
- **Splice and test.** Fusion splicing and OTDR testing, with results recorded and certified to operator standards.
- **Reinstate and hand over.** Surfaces are made good to NRSWA standard, the work is audited, and the records are handed over.

### H2: Telecoms FAQs
**What is the difference between FTTH and FTTP?**
FTTH (fibre to the home) brings fibre directly into a house. FTTP (fibre to the premises) is the wider term that also covers businesses and other buildings. Both replace copper with full fibre all the way to the building.

**Which methods do you use to install fibre?**
We choose the method to suit the route: mole ploughing for open ground and verges, directional drilling to go under roads and driveways with little surface disruption, and microduct blowing to install fibre quickly through ducts already in the ground.

**Do you test and certify your work?**
Yes. We carry out fusion splicing and OTDR testing and record the results to meet operator standards before handover.

**Which areas do you cover?**
We work across the North West, Yorkshire and the West Midlands from our base in Warrington.

**Who do you work with?**
We deliver for national operators and their partners. Our flagship programme is Virgin Media's Motif full-fibre rollout, delivered in partnership with Fujitsu. See the [Motif programme](/work/motif).

**Do you reinstate the ground after the work?**
Yes. We reinstate to NRSWA standard and audit the finished work.
